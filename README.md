# 🌱 Upliftly – Social Development Events Platform

**Upliftly** is a social development events platform that connects people who want to make a positive impact on their communities. Users can discover social events, join volunteer activities, and organize initiatives to create a better and more sustainable world.

## 🌐 Live Website

🔗 **Live Site:** [Visit Upliftly](https://upliftly-seven.vercel.app/)

## ✨ Features

- **User Authentication:** Secure registration and login system with Better Auth, including Google authentication.
- **Discover Events:** Browse and explore social development events happening in different communities.
- **Create Events:** Authenticated users can create and publish their own social events.
- **Join Events:** Users can join their favorite events and participate in community activities.
- **Event Management:** Users can view and manage the events they have created.
- **Search & Filtering:** Easily find events using search, event categories, and sorting options.
- **Dashboard Statistics:** View important statistics, including total events, upcoming events, completed events, and active volunteers.
- **Responsive Design:** Enjoy a smooth experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **User Profile:** View and update personal profile information.

## 🎯 Project Purpose

Upliftly aims to encourage social responsibility, community engagement, and volunteer participation by making it easier for people to discover and organize meaningful events.
